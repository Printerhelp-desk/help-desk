Privacy Policy

Your privacy and digital security are paramount. Learn how PrinterExpert safeguards your personal information and remote sessions.

PrinterExpert (operating at printerexpert.site, referred to herein as "PrinterExpert", "we", "us", or "our") respects your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect when you visit our website or interact with our remote printer diagnostic services, how that information is utilized, and the strict safeguards in place to protect it.

By accessing printerexpert.site or contacting our toll-free support line at +1-888-804-7080, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

Core Privacy Pledge: We never sell, lease, or distribute your personal details, phone numbers, or email addresses to third-party data brokers or marketing agencies. All remote sessions are 256-bit encrypted and initiated strictly with your affirmative consent.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to diagnose printer issues, process service transactions, and maintain effective communication:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Contact Details: Your name, phone number, and email address when you call our helpdesk or request technical assistance.
  • Hardware & Software Information: Printer model, computer operating system (Windows 11/10, macOS), network router type, and specific error codes described during technical diagnosis.
  • Billing Information: Payment details when enrolling in service plans, handled exclusively through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways. We never store full credit card numbers on our local servers.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Log Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring pages, and date/time stamps for security auditing and DDoS defense.
  • Cookies & Session Tags: Small text files placed on your device to keep track of preferences and analyze website traffic.

3. Remote Screen-Sharing Security Protocols

Our remote technician assistance relies on industry-standard, encrypted screen-sharing technologies:

  • Explicit Customer Consent: A remote session can NEVER be initiated without you manually generating or inputting a secure, one-time connection pin.
  • Full Screen Oversight: You maintain complete visual oversight of every action taken by the technician on your desktop in real-time.
  • Instant Session Termination: You have the absolute right and capability to disconnect the screen-sharing session at any moment with a single click.
  • Zero Personal File Access: Our certified technicians strictly limit their operations to printer spoolers, network IP settings, driver directories, and scanning utilities. We do not open, view, copy, or retain personal photos, banking documents, or private files.

4. How We Use Your Information

PrinterExpert uses the information collected solely for legitimate business operations:

  • To diagnose, isolate, and repair printer hardware and software errors;
  • To verify warranty coverage and provide follow-up service confirmation;
  • To communicate critical driver security updates and scheduling reminders;
  • To prevent fraudulent chargebacks, unauthorized access, and cyber threats;
  • To improve the speed and usability of our online diagnostic console.

Cookies help personalize your browsing experience and monitor server load. You may configure your web browser settings to reject optional cookies. However, disabling essential cookies may impact the interactivity of our real-time diagnostic selector.
